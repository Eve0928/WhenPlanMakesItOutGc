# 📆 When the Plan Makes it Out of the Group Chat 

Have you ever made plans with your friends, only for them to never actually happen?


One person is free on Friday, another only after 5 PM, someone else can't make it on the weekend, eventually your group chat is flooded with messages trying to find a time that works for everyone. Few minutes away from your phone, you end up receiving 50+ messages, and sometimes goes off topic. 

When the Plan Makes It Out of the Group Chat is a website designed to make scheduling with friends simple, visual, and stress-free. Instead of endless messages, everyone marks their availability in one place, and it automatically finds the best dates and times for the group.

 ## ✨✨ Key Features 

### Home Dashboard
- View all plans you're hosting or joined plans as organized cards. 
- Upload a custom cover image for each plan
### Date Availabity 
- Select available days simply by tapping on the date from a monthly calendar
### Time Selection Per Date
- Each selected date is marked as "All Day" by default. 
- If not "All Day", then simply uncheck the box and choose your availabe start & end times. 
### Color-coded 
- Each person is assigned with a color, so that others can easily see who is available on what day and what time. 

## How It Works 
1. Either create a new plan or join an existing plan using the given code
2. Select the days you're available on the monthly calendar
3. By deafult, each selected day is marked ALL Day. 
    If All Day doesn't work for you, uncheck the box and choose your preferred time range. 
4. Type in your name (or any nickname you want to be called)
5. Upload a cover for the plan (if you created)

That's it. Simple as that. Now you can see who is available and hope for the plan to finally make it out of the group chat. 

## Why This Project?
Group chats are great for conversations, but not for scheduling.

Instead of scrolling through hundreds of messages trying to figure out who is available when, this website gives everyone a single place to share their availability. The result is less communication, less confusion, less chaos, and more time actually spending time together.
